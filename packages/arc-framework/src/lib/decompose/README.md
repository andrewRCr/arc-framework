# Store decomposition

The cut map binds source content and dependency edges by stored-record identity.
`cut-map-machine.ts` defines its machine envelope and stable source, edge, and preflight identities.

`context.ts` binds all modes to one lifecycle executor's store and Git seam, together with the operator identity,
cohort template, inbound grammar, and terminal cleanup boundaries. Map inputs retain parsed JSON and its source operand.

`refusal.ts` validates mode outcomes, including text-only repairs and whole store refusals with their recovery
information lifted unchanged. `command-argv.ts` builds stored-map continuations for the shared command renderer.
`provenance.ts` distinguishes additive, terminal, discard, and revision batches in record history.

`stored-cut.ts` reads current authoring independently of start and finish admission. `discard-plan.ts` acquires
authenticated whole member groups and new cohort documents proved by `cut-cohorts.ts` against the cut's creation
history. `removal-records.ts` closes conflicts through their subjects' removals. `discard.ts` previews exact removals
and existing destinations left edited, binds approval to observed versions, revalidates, and applies only those writes.
`discard-report.ts` defines its closed preview and application reports.

`acquire-revision.ts` authenticates members once and derives fresh source facts without new-cut guards.
`revision-starter.ts` emits canonical carried maps or fresh author slots beside preserved authoring. `rebind.ts`,
`rebind-machine.ts`, and `rebind-units.ts` compare origin identity, profile, unit identity and content, repeated
headings, and stable edge identities; renamed dependents and providers receive fresh names without losing decisions.
`revision-starter-report.ts` closes the carried and reauthoring result shapes.

`bind-revision.ts` requires completed revision authoring and an exact fresh machine. `revision-admission.ts` repeats
name, allocation, home, topology, Purpose, and written-edge checks, admitting retained names only by authenticated
record reference and carrying Purpose for cut-owned documents without recreating them.

`revision-plan.ts` composes added scaffolds, whole-group removals, cohort differences, and the stored-map replacement.
`revision-members.ts` combines prior-generation settlement with retained metadata edits; `revision-seeds.ts` compares
seed providers by identity, including their retirement dispositions, while preserving unrelated edges and current aliases.
`revision.ts` binds approval to exact mutations and observed versions, applies those writes alone, and uses
`revision-recheck.ts` to report current admission failures before unexplained native refusals. `revision-report.ts`
defines its closed preview and application reports.

`removal-guards.ts` shares started-member, nested-cut, and held-entry admission with revision. `removal-holders.ts`
counts the surviving lifecycle state after prepared edits, `removal-edges.ts` protects dependency and pause holders,
and `removal-cohorts.ts` keeps documents until their open work items and child cohorts leave.

`acquire-origin.ts` retains the whole versioned origin group, projected artifact basenames, lifecycle inventory,
and stored-map observation. Incoming candidates exclude caller-supplied member UIDs while the full inventory remains
available for other checks. Start-only guards and pure machine derivation consume these observations separately.

`source-profile.ts` validates Design and Task List against stored artifact basenames. `source-facts.ts` derives the
record-bound source identity and kind, scans all planning artifacts or only an Active origin's design, and produces
canonically ordered allocation units with content digests.

`edge-facts.ts` caches identity lookups during acquisition and projects stable machine edges afterward. Incoming
dependents use current names and UIDs; outgoing providers keep authored slugs and resolve through live aliases or
terminal lineage, retaining slug identity when resolution is absent or ambiguous.

`start-guards.ts` refuses a second stored cut and held inbound entries only when starting a cut. Source conflicts,
copy prerequisites, lifecycle eligibility, and declared-profile checks stay in reusable derivation; acquisition checks
source conflicts before requiring complete inventory so a conflicted meta retains its specific repair evidence.
`machine-facts.ts` composes and authenticates the complete machine without further I/O.

`preflight.ts` repeats new-cut admission over fresh observations and emits a canonical starter map with author slots
for every source and dependency decision, without changing any record or history.

`bind-map.ts` decodes completed maps and compares their complete machine with freshly admitted preflight facts.
Wrong-shape continuations carry the supplied map operand unchanged; stale and unsupported maps name a fresh preflight.

`check-names.ts` checks member and newly created cohort names across live work items, former slugs, cohort documents,
and the map itself. It retains complete cohort observations for topology and Purpose checks, distinguishing reused open
documents from new ones and the completed-cohort exception for member names.

`check-homes.ts` resolves existing homes to open work units and applies allocation rights from active placement and
operator ownership. It retains canonical metadata identities while leaving target content and locator resolution to
the terminal batch; started meta and task-list allocations and document homes receive specific repairs.

`allocation-rules.ts` conserves destination identity, complete locator ownership, and shared-source ownership.
`topology.ts` validates placement counts and parsed cohort nesting, counting the surviving extraction origin.

`dependency-facts.ts` acquires recipient records and external provider observations. `dependency-contributions.ts`
orders all authored contributions, and `dependency-projection.ts` applies them by recipient UID, rejecting redundant
provider slots through `dependency-rules.ts` and the projection checks. Incoming replacements exclude the recipient's
canonical name and aliases before either batch. `dependency-slot.ts` preserves unrelated edge
order while replacing all origin spellings. Terminal projection emits only stub-dependent and stub-home edits.

`check-written-edges.ts` validates providers in the intended after-state and protects started recipients. Original
provider names taken by members require owner settlement before a fresh preflight or revision.

`slug-settlement.ts` prepares prior-generation stub-edge dispositions without writing them. `retired-edge.ts` follows
lineage recursively, discharges completed or Integrating providers, and retains each observed record version.
`retired-lineage.ts` resolves authenticated terminal former names; protected or unresolved edges remain for the store's
slug guard to refuse.

`check-purposes.ts` requires a substantive Purpose only for newly created cohort documents, checking a subcohort's
parent separately and directing edits to reused documents through write-back.

`member-scaffold.ts` prepares new member metadata, declared design copies, and allocated task-list or notes copies.
`scaffold-copy.ts` preserves artifact bodies while retitling the first line and rejects missing titles, task-list
creation diagnostics, and completed tasks that would prevent later removal of the copy.

`cohort-scaffold.ts` composes topology and Purpose admission with the cohort creation writer. It creates only absent
parent or child documents and supplies the validated placement to member scaffolds; at-cap fan-out waits for finishing.

`additive-plan.ts` binds and checks the completed map before composing member, cohort, prior-edge settlement, and
stored-map mutations. `slug-settler.ts` combines every settled slot on a stub into one metadata write.
`additive.ts` revalidates observations and applies the prepared lifecycle batch atomically; protected dependencies
remain for the production slug guard, with its repair preserved.

`additive-report.ts` projects the landed batch into a closed authoring report, binding member and copy references to
returned generations and preserving every allocation's source, ownership, and target locator. Existing homes receive
instructions without a content write; the next action authors destinations before previewing the stored cut.

`additive-recheck.ts` repeats binding and admission after persistence refuses, explaining competing stored cuts and
name acquisitions before returning a store failure. An unexplained version conflict retains its complete store
refusal and adds the exact additive retry action; other failures preserve their original recovery information.

`acquire-terminal.ts` acquires a stored cut's complete preparation inputs and version guards. `read-cache.ts`
shares listed records, reads, lookups, and histories within preparation; revalidation uses the original store.
`member-identity.ts` authenticates members by their birth names and creation batches against decomposition history
since the map's latest creation, sharing history reads for aliases. Accepted member generations alone leave the
incoming scan. `acquire-terminal-targets.ts` retains named cohort documents and complete open-home groups, directing
closed-home allocations through revision.

`check-terminal-origin.ts` admits the origin's current lifecycle state for the cut's shape before deriving source facts.
It repeats the shared scaffold floor and gives typed deactivation, reopening, revision, or discard actions when the
current state or name prevents finishing.

`check-member-placement.ts` validates authenticated member names and their assigned cohort, offering revision only
where one admissible placement matches every member. `check-seeded-fields.ts` retains seeded providers while allowing
other grooming and protects a retirement from member edges to the origin. `seeded-provider.ts` compares provider
owners, accepts their complete retirement dispositions through the shared chain resolver, and retains the records
that prove those decisions for revalidation.

`conservation.ts` resolves each allocation's complete saved locator against its current member, home, or cohort
record. It shares artifact scans, retains the current resolved content and selected record versions, and directs
unresolved locators to revision. Destination bodies and recomputed occurrence ordinals remain authorable.

`source-binding.ts` compares current-kind source units by identity and digest and dependencies by stable edge ID,
allowing metadata grooming, provider renames, and changes outside the current scan. Shared inbound admission in
`start-guards.ts` names held entries with their individual routing versions when a grammar is supplied.

`terminal-edges.ts` acquires current dependency recipients, prepares terminal edits, and repeats provider and
started-recipient admission with stored-cut revision remedies. Started-home intent is checked before omitted writes
can hide it; provider checks cover actual terminal additions while member seeds remain under seeded-field validation.
Written-provider refusals point to the contribution that introduced that provider, even when one edit combines edges.

`terminal-preparation.ts` shares origin, seeded-field, destination, source-binding, and recipient checks between
retirement and extraction, retaining every observed record version for application.

`lineage-plan.ts` projects admitted retirement records into create-only lineage with current names, known generations,
and the origin's exact retired branch links. Renamed dependents match by UID; current and retired provider records
retain the versions that establish their names for revalidation.

`terminal-records.ts` prepares one metadata mutation per stub recipient, explicit stored-map removal, and at-cap
fan-out append or reuse through the cohort writer. Started recipients retain their edges, and a dependent's own
stored cut remains available for revision after its provider changes. Every acquired recipient stays version-bound.

`retirement-plan.ts` composes terminal admission, content conservation, source binding, lineage, and additional writes
while retaining their version guards. `retirement-checkout.ts` finds the origin's holder and applies its marker-selected
removal or release checks before persistence, preserving projection repairs.

`retirement.ts` applies the prepared plan through the lifecycle executor, which supplies primary removal and record
revalidation. Incomplete executor inventory is read again for its typed repair; version conflicts require a fresh
finish preview. Teardown runs after persistence, surfaces preservation notices, and retains an executable continuation
when physical cleanup fails. A stub with no holder or branch link needs no physical cleanup.

`thinning.ts` derives exact retained design content from current record locators, removing an artifact that keeps no
units and omitting unchanged writes. `extraction.ts` combines thinning with recipient edits, stored-map removal,
fan-out, and the surviving origin's cohort change. It revalidates observations and applies only those mutations in
one batch, preserving companions and writing no lineage or physical cleanup effects.

`apply-authority.ts` binds removing operations to their mode, origin generation, observed record versions, and exact
prepared writes, ordering the observation and mutation sets canonically. `finish-report.ts` exposes closed preview and
application reports. `finish.ts` completes preparation and checkout admission before comparing the supplied authority;
accepted grooming requires a fresh preview and approval, and a matching authority applies exactly the previewed writes.
`extraction-finish.ts` presents retained artifact content and applies an approved surviving-origin plan. Dispatch passes
its acquired cut into the selected preparation, so choosing the shape does not repeat record acquisition.

`landed.ts` resolves a retired origin's decomposition lineage into a landed report with its recorded successors.
Finish selects a live primary first, so an item reusing a retired name keeps its own cut state. Abandonment is not a
landed cut; native resolution failures retain their recovery information, and repeated finish never writes again.

`landed-extraction.ts` reads the current origin generation's map history when no stored map remains. Its latest removal
must carry terminal decomposition provenance; the preceding map supplies historical member names. A discard removal
does not establish a landed cut, and native history failures preserve their class and repair instructions.
