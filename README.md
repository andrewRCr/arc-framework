# delivery-rebuild-continuity — reference model

An executable model, in bash and git plumbing, of the git algebra `spec-delivery-rebuild-continuity.md` settles:
construct's first cut and re-anchor, the base's recorded resolution R(B) and its attribution, which reverts the delta at
every member, the delta split and its fold at every member from the placement up — once the chain is composed without
it, and above the placed member only on that member's own paths — the contribution proof's window, and the carry across
a stopped rewrite loop's broken chain, with the source-top advance and what it passes that the member below the break
never held, read hunk by hunk against the re-anchored record and folded with the delta at the placement; the conflict
stops and the resolutions that answer them, recorded against each composition's three input trees; a stopped binding's
continuation, which binds the chain the stopped run constructed, against the rejected readings that construct it again;
and, as an optional check, the bound route's later-member stop, read at the fold, with its contribution proof. It was
built during that spec's planning to test candidate rules against concrete topologies before they were written down.

**Status.** This is evidence, not authority. The spec is the contract; where the model and the spec or the
implementation disagree, that is a finding to route, never a reason to follow the model. The model covers the git
algebra only — no Delivery State, refs, reservations, provider, or controller. It was written by the spec's author; five
independent review passes contributed cases: the eleventh `p11-a`, `p11-c`, and `p11-d` (`p11-c` since repaired: its
setup left m2's line out of the shared file, so two of its removals were empty commits), the twelfth `p12-a` through
`p12-d`, the thirteenth `x1` through `x6` with `x1b`, the fourteenth `z1` through `z3`, and the fifteenth `q1` and `q2`,
all kept as written; the fourteenth's `z2b`, which ran `z2`'s defect a second round, was not kept. The rest is the
author's own, so it catches divergence between the implementation and the model, not a misconception the two share. It
lives on this non-merging branch through the work unit's implementation and is deleted when the work unit is archived.

## Running

Requires bash and git 2.40 or later (`git merge-tree --write-tree --merge-base`).

```bash
./run.sh              # every case, from a clean state; SAME / DIFF against expected/
./run.sh p9-e p10-i   # named cases (reg/ cases as reg_<name>)
bash p10-i.sh         # one case, output to the terminal
```

Commit identity and dates are pinned (`lib1.sh`), so object ids are reproducible and `expected/` records them. Each case
builds its repositories under `r/` (or `reg/r/`); those, `results/`, and the dot-files the scripts use for scratch are
ignored.

`BREAK10=0` disables the pass-ten carry across a break, `ADV10=0` the source-top advance, and `ADV12=0` the rule for
what the advance passes that the member below the break never held; `HUNK12=0` reads that record path by path rather
than hunk by hunk. `FOLDUP=0` folds the delta at the placed member alone rather than at every member from the placement
up, and `FOLDPATH=0` folds it on every path at each member above the placement, rather than only on the paths that
member's own range changes, and drops the reapplication of each member above a waiting fold on the folded member below.
`ATTRSPLIT=0` reverts the delta's commits for attribution only at the members below the placement, and folds the delta
at the placed member as it is reapplied unless that member absorbs the base, rather than always after the chain is
composed without it; `HROLD=0` reads the hunk read's record raw, on its older base, rather than re-anchored onto the
base the members below the break absorbed; and `NOAUTH=0` counts the delta's paths as the placed member's authorship for
attribution's share too, not only for the authored-commit guard, the overlap clearing, and the beyond-base refusal. The
three are superseded readings; with all three at 0 the model reproduces every output it recorded before they were added.
With those on (the default) the model is the settled rule before publication. `RSPLIT=1`, off by default, restores the
superseded split of an uncarried part: from the break up when the member below carries part of its commit, with the
delta when it carries none. A conflict in the hunk read's second merge counts the path's change as never held, whole, so
it folds at the placement; no case reaches that branch. `CONT_TOP=<commit>`, `CONTPL=1`, and `NOTCARRIED=1` are rejected
readings of a stopped binding's continuation, kept so the cases that record them re-run. `CONT_TOP` constructs the chain
again over what the binding left, the advance first passing every first-parent commit up to the top the stopped run was
built from; `CONTPL=1` passes that top only where the placement is at or below the member below the break;
`NOTCARRIED=1` reads a conflict in the hunk read's first merge as not carried rather than carried whole. The settled
continuation binds the chain the stopped run constructed, which a case shows by constructing over that chain and
comparing object ids (`z5`, `z6`).

A conflict composing a member takes the resolution recorded for that exact composition in `RESDB` — one line per
resolution, `<base> <left> <right> <result>` as trees — or else the one the case supplies for the step: `RES<k>` for
member k's reapplication, after which its fold still runs, and `FIX<k>` for its fold, the member's content after its
whole fold. Otherwise it stops, printing the composition's key under `SHOWKEY=1`. `TERMRES=1` takes the corrected top's
normalized tree at a conflicted terminal, as construct does before publication. `BOUND=1`, off by default, adds the
bound route's later-member stop and its contribution proof. The stop is read at the fold: a fold that changes any member
above the placement stops, naming the member and the paths, whether that member absorbs the base or is carried across a
break. The proof — every member but the placed one equal to its range's three-way reapplication on its rebuilt
predecessor, or to the resolution supplied for that reapplication — is checked for members with no absorption, and for a
member carried across a break against the carry itself, which takes the attributed share of the recorded resolution the
proof admits; above the placement that carry reads attribution as the proof does, the top with only the later members'
commits reverted, and a member that differs prints `PROOF-REFUSES`. An absorbing member's proof, which admits R(B), is
not modelled. Under `ATTRSPLIT=0` only the proof check reads the stop. `run.sh` runs `q1`, `q2`, and `f6d` under it.
`q2`'s header also names `BRKCHK=1`, which no library here reads. `BTERM=1`, with `BOUND=1` and `TERMRES=1`, is the
rejected alternative `y6` records: the top's version taken at a conflicted bound terminal.

Every unbroken-chain case is byte-identical under `BREAK10=0`, and every case before `p11-*` under `ADV12=0`. `HUNK12=0`
changes only `p12-a`. `RSPLIT=1` changes `p12-b`, `x1b`, and `x1bc`: `p12-b`'s cells a, b, and d re-run a stopped
binding with another placement, which the continuation refuses, so their `NO` rows record the misplacement the
continuation prevents, and cell c, run at the stopped run's placement, is `yes` under either rule. It changes `q1`,
`q2`, and `f6d` too, where the superseded split takes the uncarried part into the terminal past the later-member stop:
`Q1b` and `f6d`'s `d3` complete where the candidates stop, and the proof refuses `Q1a` and `q2`. `FOLDPATH=0` changes
`y2`, `y3`, `y4`, `y4b`, `y5`, `y6`, `z1`, and `z5`, and the `FOLDUP=0` rows of `p12-c` and `p12-d`; and since each
member above the fold is then reapplied on the unfolded member below, on the bound route `f6a` through `f6d`, `p12-s`,
`q1`, `q2`, and `x4` take the later-member stop at m2 on the placed member's own path — in `q1`, `q2`, and `f6d` at the
loop's own construct, so what follows there is its cascade. Under `FOLDUP=0` `f6a` through `f6d`, `p11-h`, `p11-s`,
`p12-a`, `p12-b`, `p12-s`, `q1`, `q2`, `x1`, `x1b`, `x1c`, `x1bc`, `x4`, `y1`, `y2`, `y5`, `z1`, `z4`, `z5`, and
`reg/b-sources` change. `reg/b-sources` G's list-neighbours row is a negative control that reads each old member's range
from the wrong predecessor: with the fold at the placed member alone it comes out incomplete, and with the fold at every
member it comes out complete, because the later folds restore what that range reversed. `p11-n`, which runs both folds
itself, is a shape where no fold restores it. `BOUND=1` changes `p11-h`, which stops as `p12-s` records; `p11-n` and
`reg/b-sources`, whose list-neighbour controls the proof refuses too; `p12-c` and `p12-d`, whose whole-file and
whole-block controls stop; `p11-s`, every `p12-b` cell, and `p12-a`'s `b0 span` and `b1 span` cells, whose stopped run's
own rebuild stops, so on the bound route that run never happens, and the lines after that stop in those cells are its
cascade, not results; `x1`, `x1b`, `x1c`, `x1bc`, `x4`, `y1`, `y2`, `y3`, and `y4b`, the pre-publication shapes `y5`
runs on the bound route; `z4` and `z5`, whose correction reaches a later member's content, so their full rebuild takes
the later-member stop and the lines after it are its cascade; and `f6a`, `f6b`, and `f6c`, whose rows before publication
then read as their bound ones. `p12-m`, `x2`, and `x3` carry the superseded mixed check in the case itself.
`ATTRSPLIT=0` changes `f6a` through `f6d` and `q1`, whose removal of a later member's content then completes on the
bound route past the later-member stop, and `y4`, whose unresolved rows then stop at m1's fold; `HROLD=0` changes only
`q1`, whose `Q1b` completes, the hunk read counting the removal carried whole; and `NOAUTH=0` changes `f6a` and `f6b`,
which stop as an entangled chain at m1, `f6c`, which takes the departure into m1 before publication, and `q1`, whose
`Q1b` stops as an entangled chain at m2. `NOTCARRIED=1` changes `y4b`, whose recomposition then stops at m1's fold on a
composition its recorded resolution does not match, and the rejected recomposition rows of `z2`, `z3`, and `z5`;
`CONTPL=1` changes only `z2` and `z3`, whose recorded-top rows it brings equal to the full rebuild; `z5` and `z6` set it
per row.
