# Cohort: `verification-integrity`

> _Identity and coordination record for this cohort. Membership is derived from each member's `Cohort` field —
> recorded here only as shared coordination, never as a roster or status table._

**Purpose:** Make work-unit verification actively falsifying at every supported scope, then scale verifier attention
without weakening whole-concern seam coverage.

---

## Coordination

`verification-falsification-contract` owns the verification primitive's truth-seeking floor: breaking cases,
inventory completeness, and human-surface trace. `verification-scope-scaling` owns applying that primitive to
delivery members or other bounded scopes plus one whole-unit seam pass.

The two members coordinate but do not currently carry a hard dependency. Scope scaling must not duplicate the
falsification rules, and falsification must remain useful to projects that never use stacked delivery.

Success-criteria authoring mechanics remain with `planning-iteration-mechanics`; adversarial-review activity and
pass calibration remain with `review-activity-contracts`. `delivery-review-cardinality` owns provider request
cardinality, not verification scope.
