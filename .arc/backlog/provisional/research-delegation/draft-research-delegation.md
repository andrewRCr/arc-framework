## Starting Point

External research via delegated subagents is already routine in this repository's development, but it lives in
personal agent profiles and personal skills rather than ARC. The prior art is a tiered external-research shape:

- light: one bounded pass;
- medium: parallel facets with synthesis;
- heavy: verified fan-out plus adversarial checking.

ARC should decide what it owns as methodology rather than copying personal tooling wholesale.

## Questions

- Which research moments earn delegation, and which stay with the primary agent?
- How should tier choice be expressed without depending on one harness's subagent mechanics?
- What evidence and citation standards must research findings meet before they influence drafts/specs?
- How does this compose with `execution-delegation-doctrine`'s delegation rubric?
