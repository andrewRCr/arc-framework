# ARC Handoff

Apply `.arc/system/workflows/arc/session-lifecycle/session-handoff.md`. Resolve the active locus first: leave an
eligible full-mode Errand through its exact subject verb, refuse incomplete transient work, then consume the
restored parent or record-free frame. Update WU meta/SESSION-NOTES only for the restored WU, and release only its
freshly derived lease; a cold transient close has no WU lease to release.
