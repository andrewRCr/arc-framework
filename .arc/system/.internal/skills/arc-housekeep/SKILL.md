# ARC Housekeep

Apply `.arc/system/workflows/arc/supplemental/drain-inbox.md`. Confirm judgment-only dispositions, then establish
one routing occupancy with `arc housekeep open <sweep-slug> --json`. Mark all confirmed execute-now titles together,
route the fixed sweep, and close its occupancy before consuming exact next offers for sequential sibling Errands.
Abandonment preserves execute-bound markings as the next session's resume trail; an abandoned Errand clears only
its own marking. Never reopen the drain frame, split routing into another sweep, or infer restoration from the
checkout branch.
