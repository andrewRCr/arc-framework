## Release Notes Entry

Planning workflows now assess whether a candidate design uses the least elaborate credible machinery that still
satisfies its chartered goals and real constraints, catching both unsupported solution complexity and
adequacy-regressing simplification before implementation work is institutionalized.

### Added

- A configurable design-proportionality method with closed, decision-bearing findings, consequence-scaled rigor,
  existing-substrate composition, and an explicit adequacy floor.
- Proportionality coverage in the standalone design-audit door, including optional substrate referents and
  harness-wide skill projection.

### Changed

- Draft and spec authoring assess material mechanisms while designs form and at their settled boundaries; task
  generation rechecks the materialized skeleton before content fill.
- Fresh init and update installations deliver the proportionality method, design-audit skill, and every direct
  method dependency declared by the affected planning workflows.

## Completion Notes

The delivered planning judgment centralizes six proportionality questions behind one overridable method and composes
it with the existing design-fit rubric. Drafting, spec crystallization, adversarial design boundaries, the
standalone audit door, and provisional task decomposition all consume the same criterion without adding runtime
state, configuration, proof markers, transitive loading, or a new CLI surface.

The implementation preserves judgment over ceremony: material mechanisms are assessed when introduced or changed
and at the existing settled boundaries, while clean paths proceed silently. The task-generation backstop can amend
the governing spec and rebuild an invalid skeleton only through its existing approval interlock. Delivery closure
also corrected the fresh-install inventory for the planning methods those workflows already declared.

Verification passed Markdown, TypeScript, and shell linting; source and test typechecks; build; 7,802 tests; package
and project projection checks; the method-trigger audit; and four behavioral case families covering unjustified
machinery, consequence-backed complexity, and adequacy-regressing simplification.
