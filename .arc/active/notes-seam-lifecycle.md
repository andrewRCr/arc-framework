## How the rows were checked

The rows are those of `notes-storage-contract.md` § Consumer map that this work unit inherits, re-verified at
`9f5bce946` on 2026-10-12, with per-row corrections, the files no row covers, and the checks behind the design's
decisions. `generate-tasks` grounds against them and re-derives line numbers, which drift; a citation here is the file
and symbol first, its line only where the line is the evidence. A `spec §` citation names a section of
`spec-seam-lifecycle.md`.

Six read-only sweeps ran over the 79 rows and over the files no row names, each running the ratchet's own predicates
(`findArchitectureImportViolations`, `eslint/architecture-imports.ts`) and locating every floor count in its files. The
sweeps' findings are advisory. Each one the design acts on was checked against source before it entered the design;
those are marked **verified** below, and the rest stand as the sweeps reported them.

## Corrections across rows

- **Key 13 (hand-built state paths)** is missing from most rows, workflows aside. The `work-unit-paths` floor counts
  the layout-resolver calls; `join(cwd, ".arc", …)` builds and tier-prefix constants go uncounted
  (`completed-index.ts`, `lifecycle-index.ts` `TIERS`, `relocate-artifacts.ts` `PRUNE_BOUNDARY_TIERS`,
  `backlog-stub.ts`, `commands/rename.ts`'s cohort path, `transition-record-store.ts`'s
  `TRANSITION_RECORD_NAMESPACE`, `meta-reader.ts`'s `[".arc", "active"]`).
- **Key 45 (write, then `git add`)** is cited only on publish. The executor's `stageMeta` seam
  (`executor-context.ts`) and the verbs' own staging in `archive.ts`, `park-resume.ts`, and `abandon.ts`, plus the
  transition-record writer (`terminal-transition-record-writer.ts`), `executor-context-current-wu.ts`,
  `atomic-graduation.ts`, `handlers/start.ts`'s `commitAndPushStartCeremony`, `commands/rename.ts`'s `commitTracked`,
  and `park-planning-landing.ts`'s direct index write, all carry it.
- **Key 53 (`STATUS.USER` writes)** is cited nowhere: `withRender` puts `reconcile-status-user` on every edge
  (`lifecycle-transitions.ts`), and abandon and park call it directly. This member takes key 53 (spec § 2.4).
- **Key 17 and the `wu-workspace` family**: the `user-workspace` side effect is declared on most edges, and the
  workflows read and append `ADVERSARIAL-PASSES.md` in the per-work-unit workspace (draft-design, create-spec,
  generate-tasks, amend-design); no workflow row carries the family.
- **Key 14 (`plan/` branches, D17)** sits only on the deactivate row, though `plan/` coupling runs through create-spec,
  generate-tasks, activate, init, park, `commands/start.ts`, `handlers/start.ts`, `atomic-graduation.ts`,
  `git-graduation-transaction.ts`, `validated-graduation-transaction.ts`, `park-planning-landing.ts`,
  `activate-deactivate.ts`, and `promote-runtime.ts`, whose derivation-floor promotion renames the Errand branch to
  `plan/<name>` (`promotionBranch`).
- **Key 65 (`Context:` footers of planning kinds)** is missing from create-spec, generate-tasks, and amend-design,
  which the draft-design row carries for the same footer.
- **Fields retired at the flip are still fed** (`spec-seam-record-kinds.md` § 5.4): `Next Task` (activate, reopen),
  `Last Completed` (publish), and the table's soft-field resets; `Branch` and `PR URL` become links. No row says these
  writes stop at the flip.
- **`Class` is hand-edited** in draft-design's capture, activation, promotion's ratchet, and init's paths A and B,
  where no flag covers it (`arc activate` takes no `--class`; `arc promote --class` refuses a recorded value). No row
  names the Class verb.
- **Values are passed preformatted** (`formatValue` in `finalize-stage.ts`), against record kinds' rule that no caller
  formats a value; today's setters already delegate to the kind writers (`meta-reader.ts`).
- **Ratchet blind spots (verified).** `completed-index.ts` is outside the `store-raw-state` helper set, so archive's and
  teardown's reads go uncounted; the spec adds it (§ 1.4) once its pure slug helpers (`branchToWorkUnitSlug`,
  `completedWorkUnitMetaSlug`) move out, since eleven files import it for those alone. The task-list path comes through
  an upward import of `resolveTaskListPath` from `commands/active/status.ts`, with eight production importers: publish,
  reopen, `subject-meta.ts` (locus), `handlers/view.ts` (status), `delivery-execution.ts`,
  `lifecycle-delivery-review.ts`, `checkpoint-composition.ts`, and `validate-meta-spec.ts` (removed at the cutover).
  `readiness-regen.ts`'s three surface-name counts are message text, not paths, so its floor overstates it.

## Checks behind the design

- **When the executor closes the workspace (verified).** `user-workspace` runs `runUserClose` whenever the transition
  leaves `active`, among the side effects, before the finalize writes (`Branch`, soft fields, `Current Workflow`,
  `stageMeta`); a throw there returns `finalize-failed` with the close recorded, and `runUserClose` is idempotent. Live
  exposure is archive alone: park from Planning defers the close until retirement completes, park from Active closes
  after its pointer write, and abandon swaps in a no-op and closes after teardown.
- **Which guard refuses a park or abandon (verified).** `worktree-clean` (`makeWorktreeCleanGuard`) is declared only on
  park from Active's edge, and `runPark` sends that case to `parkActive`, which calls `reconcileWorkUnitWorktree`
  directly, so the guard never runs. The refusal that happens is the mutator's clean check before teardown and again
  under the locked-retirement driver (`reconcile-work-unit-worktree.ts`). No abandon edge declares a clean guard, and no
  abandon path refuses a dirty checkout before it writes.
- **Every checkout and branch teardown removes (verified for the checkout removals).** Checkouts: a shipped linked
  checkout through `reconcileWorkUnitWorktree`; a self or abandoned linked checkout detached and stamped as a husk; a
  husk's later removal with `huskApproved`; the in-place primary switched to base, never removed; and the decomposition
  candidate's direct `git worktree remove` (`cleanupGitOwnedDecomposeCandidate`), which restores ARC-written files first
  and refuses user content. Branches: a leased remote delete and a compare-and-swap local delete on the authorized path,
  `delete-merged` and a contained remote delete for shipped work without authority, refusal otherwise, then
  remote-tracking prune; the candidate's `update-ref -d`.
- **Other worktree removals ARC runs (verified).** Spawn rollbacks (`rollbackSpawnedWorktree`, `rollbackFreshSpawn`,
  graduation's and provisioning's), the Errand exit (`terminal-occupancy.ts`), delivery's gate-checkout reap
  (`residue-reaping.ts`), base sync's managed worktree, and the review gate's local review checkouts.
- **Reopen (verified).** It reads the `active/` meta and the task list, dispatches the one edge out of Integrating, and
  never touches `completed/`; an archived unit matches no edge.
- **Archive numbering.** `computeArchiveDestination` takes the quarter from the clock, reads this checkout's copy of the
  quarter folder, counts the cohort sidecars, and pads the highest plus one to two digits, with no collision check. The
  cohort sidecars borrow the member's number as `a` (leaf) and `b` (parent).
