# Notes: storage-contract

> _Companion to `draft-storage-contract.md`. It holds the consumer map's inventory; the draft's § The consumer map
> holds the map's design layer and the decisions it raised._

## Consumer map

Every ARC reader and writer of operational and planning state, one row per module, command, workflow, method,
extension, skill, hook, or script, grouped by the `storage-seam` partition that owns its rewrite. `storage-seam`
decomposes against it; its members inherit these rows and re-derive line-level detail when they plan.

### Columns

- **Consumer** — code under `packages/arc-framework/` as `src/...`; everything else from the repository root.
- **Kind** — module, command, workflow, method, extension, skill, hook, script, template, or config.
- **Owner** — `seam` (`storage-seam`, rerouted), `cutover` (removed at cutover), `ref-backend`, `projection`, or the
  follow-on work unit a register row names. A workflow, method, extension, or skill row names the member whose verbs
  it calls; its text changes once, in `storage-cutover`'s flip rewrite (`draft-storage-contract.md` § What this work
  unit lands).
- **Register** — register rows by key (§ Register keys).
- **Families** — C2's families, with the work-unit family split by kind (`wu.meta`, `wu.tasks`, `wu.prose`,
  `wu.inbound`), plus three that are not families: `placement` (lifecycle read from directory placement or branch
  refs), `derived` (ROADMAP, `STATUS.USER`, the archive index), and `machine-local`.
- **Operations** — C1's `read`, `list`, `write` (compare-and-swap), `batch` (several records, all or nothing),
  `history`, `version` (the store-wide state version), `at` (read or list as of a version), `changes` (between two
  versions), `sync`, `lookup` (reverse lookup), and `migrate`; and two projection queries, `path` (a record's
  projected path, through the layout resolver) and `pending` (unpersisted edits in the projection). `batch`,
  `version`, `at`, and `changes` are tagged on the consumers the sweep's findings named; others may need them too.
- **Reads** — `store` for a deciding consumer (a transition, guard, refusal, claim, or merge), which must read the
  store; `projection` for a browse, render, or advisory reader; `both` where a consumer does each.
- **Becomes** — what it turns into through the contract, as the sweep read it; `?` marks a guess. It is the end
  state: where that takes effect at the flip, today's behavior holds until then.
- **Shared with** — other partitions whose rewrite edits the same file.
